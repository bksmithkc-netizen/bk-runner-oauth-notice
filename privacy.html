Privacy and Google Data Use

Updated November 7, 2026.

BK Runner is an internal tool operated for Brenton Smith and Elite Commercial Roofing. Access is limited to accounts whose owners authorize the relevant connection.

Data accessed and purpose

With authorization, the runner reads Elite Gmail messages and attachments for job intake and follow-up, and reads or writes approved Elite Calendar events for business scheduling. A separate alerts connection sends notices only from Brenton’s email address to that same address. Separately authorized personal Gmail and Calendar access is read-only and used for private organization.

Processing, storage and sharing

Selected content may be processed through the OpenAI API for focused interpretation or summaries. Operational facts and results are retained in the protected server and the relevant authorized Notion or JobTread records. Personal details stay with their private owners and are excluded from shared operational logs. Credentials are kept in protected server storage.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. It is used only for the authorized functions described here. BK Runner does not use Google data to develop or train generalized AI models. Its use and transfer of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

Control and contact

Owners may turn off runner jobs and revoke access through their Google Account’s connected-app settings. Contact Brenton at bksmithkc@gmail.com for questions or requests to review, correct or remove stored data.
