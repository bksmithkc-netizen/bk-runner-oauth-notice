BK Runner

BK Runner is private automation for Brenton Smith and Elite Commercial Roofing. It is not a public service or an application offered to customers.

When authorized, the runner reads roofing correspondence and job records, organizes internal follow-up, supports estimating, and maintains approved business-calendar events. Proposals remain unsent for human review.

The runner uses separate, scoped Google connections. Elite email access is read-only. Calendar changes are limited by the application to approved Elite events. The alerts connection sends system notices only to Brenton’s own email address. Personal reading is enabled separately, only when authorized.
